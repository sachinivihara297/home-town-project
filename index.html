About the town

Discover Puttalam

Puttalam is a historic coastal town in Sri Lanka's North Western Province, built around the wide waters of Puttalam Lagoon. For generations the town has grown around fishing, salt production, agriculture and trade, shaped by the rhythm of the coast and the mix of communities who call it home.

From the lagoon's shifting light to the daily bustle of its markets, Puttalam carries a coastal identity that is both working and welcoming — a town of livelihoods, landscapes and long-shared history.

The story so far

A Journey Through Time

The timeline below traces the broad chapters of Puttalam's development — from early settlement along the lagoon to the working coastal town it is today.

  1. 01

    Early Settlement

    Puttalam developed around its coastal and lagoon environment, where fishing, salt and other natural resources supported early settlement. Historical traditions connect the wider region with Uruwela and the ancient pearl trade, linking Puttalam to the economic networks of early Sri Lanka.

  2. 02

    Maritime Connections

    Puttalam’s coastal and lagoon setting connected the town with maritime trade routes across the northwestern coast. Its links with Indian Ocean traders, pearl fisheries and neighbouring ports helped establish Puttalam as an important gateway for the movement of people and goods..

  3. 03

    Trade & Salt

    Puttalam developed as a trading centre linked to pearls, cinnamon, areca nut, coconut and imported goods. Its coastal environment also supported salt production, which became one of the town’s defining economic activities and continues to shape Puttalam’s identity.

  4. 04

    Colonial Period

    Portuguese, Dutch and British rule transformed Puttalam’s role as a coastal trading centre. During the Dutch and British periods, canals and other transport infrastructure strengthened connections between Puttalam, neighbouring coastal settlements and Colombo, supporting the movement of salt, pearls, cinnamon, coconut and other goods.

  5. 05

    Modern Puttalam

    Following independence, improved road and infrastructure networks strengthened Puttalam’s regional role. The development of the A3 and A12 routes and the establishment of Puttalam as an Urban Council in 1978 supported its transformation into a growing administrative and service centre.

  6. 06

    Puttalam Today

    Today, Puttalam is a diverse coastal urban centre where history, trade, culture and the lagoon environment continue to shape everyday life. Its strategic road connections, economic activities and ongoing development projects are strengthening its role as a regional service and commercial centre.

The lifeblood of the town

The Heart of Puttalam

Puttalam Lagoon is the ecological and economic centre of the town — a wide, shallow body of water fringed by mangroves and wetlands. It supports fishing livelihoods, shelters birds and marine life, and shapes the identity of every community that lives along its shore. Its scale and biodiversity also give it real, if still developing, tourism potential — and make its environmental health a shared local priority.

Where to go

Places That Define Puttalam

Puttalam Lagoon

A vast coastal lagoon known for its rich biodiversity, extensive mangrove ecosystems, and vibrant fishing communities. It is famous for salt production, prawn farming, and popular water sport activities such as kite surfing towards Kalpitiya.

Discover

Puttalam Clock Tower

A prominent historic landmark situated at a central intersection in the heart of Puttalam town. Surrounded by busy commercial streets, it serves as an iconic meeting point, navigation landmark, and a symbol of the town's heritage.

Discover

Puttalam Railway Station

2. Puttalam Railway Station The terminus of the Puttalam Line operated by Sri Lanka Railways, originally opened in 1926. It plays a key role in connecting the coastal town to Colombo and intermediate towns for passenger travel and freight transport. 

Discover

Puttalam Bus Stand

The central transport hub located in the heart of Puttalam town, operating both SLTB and private bus routes. It connects commuters directly to major cities across Sri Lanka, including Colombo, Kurunegala, Chilaw, and Jaffna..

Discover

Puttalam Grand Mosque (Mohideen Jumma Masjid)

A landmark Islamic place of worship with origins dating back to the late 15th century. Rebuilt in 1938 with inspiration from Egyptian Islamic architecture, it features a century-old clock imported from Rome and stands as a symbol of the town's rich cultural history. 

Discover

Salt Production Areas

Vast coastal salt pans—including major sites like Palaviya—that take advantage of the region’s dry climate and strong winds to produce table salt via solar evaporation. The vast, shimmering waters present an industrial landscape that is also popular for birdwatching along the lagoon edge

Discover

Puttalam Fish Market

A bustling early-morning hub located along the lagoon coast where local fishermen unload their daily catch. It offers a vibrant display of fresh seafood, prawns, and crabs. 

Discover

Puttalam Vegetable Market.

The town’s central produce market, packed with local farmers and traders offering fresh fruits, vegetables, and spices sourced from the surrounding agrarian regions. It serves as a vital daily trading spot for residents, wholesale buyers, and local restaurant owners.

Discover

Puttalam Bauddha Madhyasthanaya (Buddhist Cnter)

A prominent Buddhist religious and community center situated within Puttalam town. It serves as a spiritual site for worship, Dhamma education, and local cultural gatherings for the area's Buddhist population.

Discover

Open University of Sri Lanka.

A regional study center situated along Colombo Road that provides accessible distance-learning options for higher education. It offers working adults and local students various certificate, diploma, and degree programs

Discover

Puttalam Base Hospital.

A major government healthcare institution serving the Puttalam District with specialized medical, surgical, maternity, and emergency care. It acts as a primary healthcare hub for local communities and surrounding rural divisions. .

Discover

Puttalam Ongoing Radar Project.

A state-of-the-art Doppler Weather Radar project being built by the Department of Meteorology in partnership with JICA. Once completed, it will provide high-precision, real-time rainfall monitoring and early warnings to enhance climate resilience across Sri Lanka.

The wider landscape

Nature Around Puttalam

Beyond the town, Puttalam is surrounded by mangroves, wetlands and coastal land that support birds, fishing livelihoods and agriculture a landscape as working as it is beautiful.

Protecting Our Home

Like many coastal towns, Puttalam faces real environmental challenges,lagoon pollution, waste management, seasonal flooding, pressure on biodiversity, and the longer-term effects of climate change. Local and community-led efforts toward sustainable development are helping protect the lagoon and wetlands for the generations that depend on them.
